# Stanford HAI AI Index 2026 — Policy and Governance Chapter

## Précis

The Policy and Governance chapter of Stanford HAI's 2026 AI Index Report provides quantitative data on global AI governance trends through 2025. Six headline findings illuminate the current governance landscape. First, the fastest growth in national AI strategies is occurring in countries with no formal AI policy five years ago — over half of newly adopted 2024 strategies came from emerging economies. Second, **AI sovereignty** — the goal of gaining domestic agency over AI capabilities — has emerged as a central principle of national AI policy, though the infrastructure to achieve it (compute clusters, data infrastructure) is highly concentrated in Europe and North America. Third, regional approaches to data sovereignty diverge sharply: East Asia-Pacific leads in data localization measures (77 through 2024), while North America has only 3. Fourth, AI-related witnesses in U.S. congressional hearings have grown twentyfold since 2017 (5→102), with industry now the largest witness group (37%). Fifth, U.S. public AI investment ($20.4B over 2013–2024) is dwarfed by private investment ($285.9B in 2025 alone). Sixth, European public AI commitments ($3.7B over 2013–2024) are accelerating — the UK committed 28% of its decade total in 2024 alone.

These data points collectively tell a story about AI governance's political economy: governance capacity is unequally distributed; private capital vastly outpaces public investment in the U.S.; legislative interest is rising sharply; and the geopolitics of AI is increasingly defined by sovereignty concerns rather than purely technical or safety concerns. The concentration of AI infrastructure in Europe and North America while governance interest globalizes creates a structural tension — countries developing AI policy may have limited ability to shape the AI systems they are trying to govern.

**Note:** This source contains the six key findings from the chapter's landing page only; the full chapter data underlying each finding is in the complete report.
