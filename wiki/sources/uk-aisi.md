# UK AI Security Institute (AISI)

## Précis

The UK AI Security Institute (AISI) — rebranded from "AI Safety Institute" during 2024 — is the first state-backed organization explicitly dedicated to advancing scientific understanding of risks from advanced AI and translating that understanding into policy-relevant guidance. Established in the wake of the Bletchley Summit and initially housed in the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology, AISI has grown to over 100 technical staff, including alumni from OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and Oxford. Its mandate spans technical research (capability evaluation, risk assessment, development of safeguards), global coordination (information-sharing with allied governments, research MOUs with major developers including Google DeepMind), and policy informing.

AISI's institutional significance for AI governance is as a proof-of-concept that governments can build credible technical capacity for frontier AI evaluation — bridging the expertise gap that has historically left regulators dependent on industry self-assessment. Its published work includes a Frontier AI Trends Report (an evidence-based assessment of advanced AI system evolution synthesizing two years of internal model testing) and research on AI persuasion capabilities (a large-scale experiment on persuasion mechanisms published in *Science*). The name change from "Safety" to "Security" reflects a political rebranding toward national security framing, though the substantive research agenda has remained focused on safety evaluation and capability assessment. AISI's model of government-funded technical evaluation has influenced analogous institutions in the United States (NIST AI Safety Institute), the EU (AI Office), and other allied governments.

## Key terms introduced

- Frontier AI evaluation — the practice of systematically testing advanced AI models to characterize capabilities and risks before and after deployment
- Government technical capacity — state-funded expertise enabling independent (non-industry) evaluation of frontier AI systems
