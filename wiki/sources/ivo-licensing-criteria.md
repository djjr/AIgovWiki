# IVO Licensing Criteria — Design Menu

## Précis

Two working documents (Dan Ryan, IASEAI workshop series) that enumerate design choices for an AI safety IVO licensing system. The checklist is the compact decision-menu version; the exhaustive list is the comprehensive catalog. Both are working documents in draft status, not finalized proposals.

The licensing criteria are organized around four lifecycle stages: Entry (what must organizations demonstrate to get licensed?), Scope (what activities are authorized?), Ongoing oversight (how is licensee performance monitored?), and Enforcement (what sanctions apply for non-compliance?). Entry requirements span organizational structure and governance, independence and conflict-of-interest policies, quality management systems, financial stability, qualified personnel credentials, technical competence in AI safety domains, and prior track record.

The most AI-specific challenge surfaced by both documents is the "qualified personnel" criterion: what credentials constitute competence to evaluate frontier AI safety? No established professional certification analogous to CPA (audit) or PE (engineering) exists for AI safety evaluation. The documents flag this as a bootstrapping problem for any IVO licensing system.
