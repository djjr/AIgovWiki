# IVO Regulatory Table — Cross-Industry Comparison

## Précis

A comparative reference table covering 17 third-party regulatory service provider types across 10 industry domains: product safety (NRTLs, EU Notified Bodies), financial services (PCAOB audit firms, NRSROs), food and agriculture (USDA organic certifiers, HACCP auditors), environment (vehicle emissions stations, NELAP environmental labs), healthcare (Joint Commission, FDA third-party device reviewers, clinical lab accreditors), transportation infrastructure (bridge inspectors, pipeline integrity firms), aviation (FAA DERs, FAA Part 145 repair stations), building/construction (licensed inspectors), and maritime (classification societies), and nuclear (Authorized Nuclear Inspection Agencies / ANIs). For each, the table identifies the empowering authority, mechanism of empowerment, and key oversight conditions.

The table's primary value is comparative: it shows the range from direct government licensing of individuals (FAA DERs, where the FAA appoints individual engineers, not firms) through accreditation-based recognition (NELAP labs, USDA certifiers, where a standards body issues accreditation and government recognizes it) to formal designation (EU Notified Bodies, maritime Recognized Organizations, where government issues a formal designation under international legal frameworks). The pipeline integrity assessment field is a notable exception: no formal firm-level licensing exists; operators are responsible for using qualified personnel, and government audits operators rather than firms directly.
