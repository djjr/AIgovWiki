# Executive Order 14179: Removing Barriers to American Leadership in AI (Trump, 2025)

## Précis

Executive Order 14179, signed January 23, 2025, revoked EO 14110 and replaced its governing framework with a single overriding objective: sustaining and enhancing American global AI dominance. The order is notable for its brevity (four substantive sections), its explicit framing of the prior order's provisions as ideologically biased barriers to innovation, and its absence of any safety, equity, or worker-protection language. It directs the development of an AI Action Plan within 180 days and instructs agencies to review, suspend, or rescind any actions taken under EO 14110 that are inconsistent with the new policy.

The theoretical and policy significance of EO 14179 lies in what it removes rather than what it establishes. It eliminates federal reporting requirements for dual-use foundation model training, suspends work on red-teaming guidelines and biosecurity frameworks, and reverses the principle that safety is a precondition for deployment. The replacement framing — AI "free from ideological bias or engineered social agendas" — signals a fundamental reorientation from safety-and-equity to competitiveness-and-deregulation as the organizing logic of U.S. AI policy. This creates a structural divergence from the EU AI Act, the Bletchley/Seoul commitments, and the NIST RMF that subsequent policymakers will need to navigate.

EO 14179 is best understood in conjunction with EO 14110 as a paired illustration of how AI governance can swing when embedded in partisan politics rather than statutory law — a contrast often drawn with the EU's more durable legislative approach.

## Key terms introduced

- AI sovereignty framing — "sustain and enhance America's global AI dominance" as the animating policy principle; contrast with EU's "trustworthy AI" framing
