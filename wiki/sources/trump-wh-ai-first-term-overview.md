## Précis

This is the archived AI.gov portal from Trump's first term, summarizing the American AI Initiative (EO 13859, February 2019) and its implementation through January 2021. The page is a primary-source snapshot of first-term US AI policy framing: the governing metaphor is American economic and national security leadership, with innovation and deregulation as the primary policy instruments. The five pillars of the American AI Initiative — increasing federal AI research investment, unleashing federal computing and data resources, setting AI technical standards, building the AI workforce, and engaging international allies — were codified into law via the National AI Initiative Act of 2020.

First-term accomplishments logged on the site include: doubling non-defense AI R&D commitments, establishing the first national AI research institutes (NSF-led, $140M), releasing world's first federal AI regulatory guidance (light-touch, pro-innovation), launching the OECD AI Principles in May 2019 (a multilateral first), co-founding the Global Partnership on AI (GPAI) at G7 in May 2020, and establishing the National Artificial Intelligence Initiative Office (NAIIO) in January 2021. The framing is consistently competitive (vs. China) and pro-private-sector. Safety is mentioned primarily as a sub-theme of the AI standards and trustworthy AI strands, not as a primary organizing principle.

The historical value of this source for the wiki is as a baseline for understanding what the Trump administration's AI posture was before Biden's EO 14110 (2023) and what the second term's EO 14179 (2025) returned to — framing continuity between the two Trump terms alongside the interruption. The document also records the international multilateral infrastructure the US was actively building in 2019–2021, which Biden continued and Trump's second term has treated more ambivalently.

## Key terms introduced

- American AI Initiative (EO 13859) — the organizing framework of Trump first-term AI policy
- GPAI (Global Partnership on AI) — multilateral body co-founded at G7 May 2020
- NAIIO (National Artificial Intelligence Initiative Office) — federal coordination hub, established Jan 2021

## Tensions / contradictions with existing wiki

- The first-term portal shows US actively building multilateral AI infrastructure (OECD principles, GPAI, G7/G20 adoption) — a stark contrast with the second term's more unilateral "America First" framing and the December 2025 preemption EO.
- The deregulatory stance on AI was continuous across both terms, but first-term safety language (NIST standards, trustworthy AI, ethical principles) is more present than in second-term documents.
