# IVO Catalog of Engagement Modes

## Précis

A taxonomy of IVO interaction types derived from cross-industry analogues (NRTLs, Notified Bodies, PCAOB audit firms, NRSROs, USDA certifying agents, emissions stations, environmental labs, Joint Commission, FAA DERs, FAA Part 145 repair stations, classification societies, and nuclear ANIs). The catalog is organized in two parts: IVO interactions with the regulatee, and IVO interactions with the government authority.

Seven interaction modes with the regulatee are identified: (1) Engagement initiation / market selection — how the regulatee selects an IVO; (2) Application and documentation submission — the regulatee's primary act of disclosure; (3) On-site physical inspection — scheduled, periodic, or unannounced; (4) Testing and technical evaluation — standardized tests applied against defined criteria; (5) Embedded or hold-point oversight — IVO personnel inside the regulatee's processes at mandatory control points; (6) Certification, opinion, or rating issuance — the formal determination; (7) Ongoing surveillance and market monitoring — post-certification monitoring for compliance drift.

Mode 5 (embedded/hold-point) is flagged as the highest-intensity model, requiring the most structural independence. Mode 6 creates an inherent fee-for-certificate tension. The catalog's value for AI IVO design is in mapping which combination of modes is appropriate for which AI development activity: pre-training evaluation may require Mode 2 and 4; capability evaluations at capability thresholds may require Mode 5; deployment monitoring requires Mode 7.
